Questions

Things sellers ask us

Do I need to install an app?

No. Your customers chat as normal. You send photos to one WhatsApp number and look at your dashboard in the browser.

How does it know a payment really arrived?

It matches the amount and the order number, and it checks the time. A screenshot alone is not treated as proof, so anything unclear comes to you with the likely orders to pick from.

What if the bot does not know the answer?

It stops and tells you. Angry customers, refund requests and anything outside your rules go to you, and the customer is told you will reply.

Does it work with cash on delivery?

Yes. It tracks how much cash each courier holds, then checks every payout sheet against the delivered orders and flags anything short.

Can I add my own costs?

Yes. Add a category like packing, photoshoot or part-time helper. Set it per parcel, as a share of sales, or as a fixed monthly amount.

What does it not do?

It does not post on social media, run your ads or work as a cashier in a physical shop. It does the accountant's job and the support person's job for your online store.
